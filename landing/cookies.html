Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026 · Applies to the Roomy marketing website.

This Cookie Policy explains how Azbouki uses cookies and similar technologies on the Roomy marketing website (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

The Roomy macOS application does not set the cookies described below. This policy applies only to visitors of the Site.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as local storage) may be used for the same purposes. Cookies can be "session" (deleted when you close the browser) or "persistent" (remain until they expire or you delete them).

2. How we use cookies

We group cookies on the Site as follows:

Category Purpose Provider Typical duration
Essential Remember your cookie consent choice so we do not ask again on every visit. Azbouki (local storage key roomy_cookie_consent_v1) Until you clear site data or change preferences
Analytics (optional) Measure page views and how visitors use the Site (e.g. which pages are viewed, approximate location derived from IP, browser type). Google Analytics (Google LLC), only if you accept optional cookies See Google documentation; commonly up to 24 months depending on configuration

Examples of analytics cookies set by Google Analytics may include _ga and _ga_*.

3. Legal basis (EEA / UK)

Essential cookies are used based on our legitimate interest in operating the Site and recording your consent. Analytics cookies are used only if you opt in via the cookie banner (consent). You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 5).

4. Third parties

  • Google Analytics — processes usage data on our behalf when you accept analytics cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Firebase Hosting — hosts the Site and may process standard server logs (IP address, requested URL, user agent) as part of delivery.
  • jsDelivr — delivers web fonts; your browser may send request metadata to that CDN.

5. Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner on first visit: Accept all, Reject optional, or Manage to toggle analytics only.
  • Change your mind anytime: Open cookie preferences.
  • Block or delete cookies via your browser settings. If you reject analytics cookies, we do not load Google Analytics for that browser.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics across sites: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. California notice

If you are a California resident, our Privacy Policy describes the categories of information collected on the Site and how to contact us. We do not sell personal information for money. Analytics data may be shared with Google as a service provider when you consent to analytics cookies.
