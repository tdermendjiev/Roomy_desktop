Nothing gets deleted without your say.

Browse every project and system cache, understand every artifact, deselect anything you're not sure about. Roomy shows you exactly what will be removed before you commit — project files go to Trash by default; Docker and Conda cleanup runs via their native CLIs.

  • Every artifact explained in plain language with safety badges
  • Remove individual items from the deletion list at any time
  • Project artifacts move to Trash — restoreable before you empty it
  • Extra warnings for irreversible items like Xcode Archives and Docker volumes

Safe by design Project artifacts use NSWorkspace.recycle() — the macOS Trash API. Irreversible items get an extra warning before deletion. Docker and Conda cleanups use their official CLI tools, not Trash.

Does it work on Apple Silicon?

Do I need an account?

No. Download and run. Nothing to sign up for.

What are system caches?

Fixed-path developer folders outside your projects — Xcode DerivedData, global npm/pip caches, Homebrew, Docker, and more. Roomy scans them in a separate pass and groups them at the top of your results.

Is deleting DerivedData safe?

Yes. Xcode rebuilds DerivedData on the next build. Roomy labels it Safe and explains what each cache does before you delete anything.
